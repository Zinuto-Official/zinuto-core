# Contributor License Agreement

Zinuto uses a Contributor License Agreement because the same source is offered
under GPL-3.0-only and separate commercial licenses.

- Individuals sign `docs/legal/ICLA.md` version 1.0.
- Organizations sign `docs/legal/CCLA.md` version 1.0 and identify an authorized
  representative.
- The checked-in acknowledgement registry is
  `contributors/cla-signatures.json`. It will become public with the
  repository.

Adding an entry means the contributor has read the applicable agreement,
accepts it for the contribution in that pull request and future contributions
until withdrawal for future work, and confirms the recorded GitHub account is
theirs or is controlled by the named organization.

Withdrawal does not revoke rights already granted for accepted contributions.
The company keeps non-public executed copies and corporate authorization
evidence where required.
