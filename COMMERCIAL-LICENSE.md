# Commercial licensing

Zinuto Core Desktop is available under two licensing paths:

1. **GPL-3.0-only.** You may inspect, modify, build, fork, and redistribute the
   software under the GPL, including for commercial purposes.
2. **Commercial license.** Organizations that need to combine Zinuto with
   closed-source software, distribute a proprietary derivative, or deliver an
   OEM product without GPL obligations can obtain a separate written license
   from 轻创掘金（青岛）信息技术有限公司.

The commercial license changes the software-license obligations. It does not
unlock product features, create an account, enable cloud functionality, or
grant the Zinuto trademark and logo. Trademark/OEM branding requires an
explicit additional grant.

Community users never need a commercial license merely to use the application,
operate a business with it, sell GPL-compliant copies, or publish a renamed GPL
fork.

The commercial agreement is not stored in this source repository. Current
contact details are published on https://www.zinuto.com/.
