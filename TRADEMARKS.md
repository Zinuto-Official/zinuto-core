The source code is licensed under GPL-3.0-only and is being prepared for public
release. The official identity is not open source.

"Zinuto", "Zinuto Core", their respective logos and icons, and other brand
assets identify releases controlled by 轻创掘金（青岛）信息技术有限公司. The
GPL license for the code does not grant a trademark license.

## Allowed without separate permission

- Truthful, nominative references such as "a fork of Zinuto" or "compatible
  with Zinuto".
- Links to the official project, documentation, or reviewed source tags.
- Distribution of an exact, unmodified local build of a reviewed `Zinuto Core`
  source tag, provided its source-build status, license notices, and
  non-official status remain clear.
- Screenshots used for commentary, teaching, reporting, or review, provided
  they do not imply sponsorship.

## Permission required

- Distributing a modified application under the name "Zinuto" or `Zinuto Core`.
- Using either Zinuto logo or application icon in a modified redistributed build.
- Using an official Bundle ID, package identity, signing identity, update
  endpoint, telemetry endpoint, or store listing.
- Claiming that a fork, OEM build, service, or installer is official, approved,
  certified, or supported by Zinuto.

Forks intended for distribution must replace the default `Zinuto Core` source
identity with their own name, icon, package IDs, signing identities, update
infrastructure, and support contact. The internal `community` profile identifies
the upstream source composition; it is not a company-signed release, generic
rebranding profile, or trademark grant.

Commercial software licensing and trademark/OEM branding are separate grants.
Contact the company through the commercial licensing channel listed on
https://www.zinuto.com/ for written permission.
