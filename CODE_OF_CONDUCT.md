# Code of conduct

Zinuto Core should be a place where people can ask, learn, disagree, and build
without being belittled. This applies to maintainers, contributors, reviewers,
and anyone participating in project spaces.

## Expected conduct

- Discuss the work, not a person's worth or motives.
- Be specific when reporting a problem or reviewing a change.
- Make room for people with different languages, backgrounds, and levels of
  experience.
- Accept correction, acknowledge mistakes, and help repair their effects.
- Protect private information. Do not post credentials, personal market data,
  private messages, or another person's identifying details.

## Unacceptable conduct

Harassment, threats, sexualized attention, discriminatory remarks, deliberate
intimidation, doxxing, sustained disruption, and retaliation are not accepted.
Neither is using a technical discussion to attack or humiliate someone.

Good-faith disagreement is welcome. Repeating an argument after maintainers
have closed it, flooding a thread, or moving an unwanted exchange to another
channel is disruptive behavior.

## Scope and enforcement

This policy applies in repository discussions, issues, pull requests, reviews,
and project events. It also applies when someone is speaking as a project
representative.

Maintainers may edit or remove content, pause a discussion, restrict
participation, or ban a participant when needed to protect the community. The
response should be proportionate to the behavior and its effect.

To report conduct privately, use the [official contact
page](https://www.zinuto.com/en/contact/) and include links or screenshots that
help explain what happened. Do not use a public issue for a report involving
personal information. Reports are handled as privately as practical, but a
specific outcome or response time cannot be promised.
